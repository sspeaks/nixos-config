## Universe

## Team Ethos

- **No incumbency bias.** The existing config (Catppuccin Mocha, Hyprland, waybar/wofi/dunst, JetBrainsMono, current module layout) has no special standing. Every choice is judged on merit against real alternatives, and bold replacements are welcome.
- **Particularly opinionated.** Each member holds and defends a clear design position — strong opinions, loosely held. Morpheus arbitrates taste; Fact Checker and Rai keep it honest.
